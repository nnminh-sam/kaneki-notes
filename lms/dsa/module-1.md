# Module 1: Complexity Analysis & Architectural Trade-offs
## Time and Space Complexity (Big O)
- $O(1)$ Constant
- $O(\log n)$ Logarithmic
- $O(n)$ Linear
- $O(n \log n)$ Linearithmic
- $O(n^2)$ Quadratic
## Architectural Trade-offs
- Time vs Space: Trading RAM for speed.
- Read vs Write: Optimizing for the most frequent operation.
